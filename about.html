Hello there!

I’m Christopher Laflamme, a graphic designer recently graduated with a Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design from the University of Quebec in Montreal (UQAM). Before that, I completed my Graphic Design Diploma at Dawson College in 2019.

Since then, I’ve combined freelancing with expanding my skills through my studies and professional projects. My passion for design goes way back, ever since childhood, I was fascinated by logos I spotted around the city and would try recreating them. In high school, a friend introduced me to Adobe Photoshop, and that’s where my hands-on journey began. A key influence on my artistic growth is my cousin, a Montreal-based urban artist, who taught me to draw characters with accurate proportions and respect for dimension. Design has truly been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

Today, I focus primarily on branding, print, and environmental design, but I’ve also developed a strong interest in physical projects, building furniture, creating immersive experiences, and crafting visuals through photography. I’m always eager to push my digital and illustrative skills further while exploring these more tactile dimensions of design.

Attention to detail and dedication guide my process from research to final execution. I believe in strong collaboration and clear communication to ensure clients are part of every step, turning their vision into reality. Looking ahead, I aspire to join a dynamic design studio or agency where I can continue to grow as a designer and contribute fresh ideas.

I’m excited about the possibility of working with you to bring fresh ideas and innovative design solutions to life.

Thanks for stopping by, and I look forward to connecting!
