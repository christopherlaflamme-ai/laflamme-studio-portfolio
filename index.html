About me

Currently working as a Freelance Designer.

I’m Christopher Laflamme, a Montreal-based graphic designer with a passion that started in childhood and never stopped growing. With a degree from UQAM and a background in freelancing, I focus on branding, print, and spatial design—while also exploring hands-on projects like furniture, immersive spaces, and photography. I love crafting thoughtful visuals and collaborating to bring bold ideas to life.
