<p>The goal of this project was to create a user-friendly and practical bottle that is easy to clean while maintaining a modern and minimalist look. Building upon this idea, the next step was to develop a brand image around this product. To my surprise, the name "Botl" had not been used by any company to date.</p><p>‍</p><p>Not everyone has the budget to purchase a washing machine specifically for cleaning reusable bottles like ours. That's why I came up with the idea of a reusable bottle with a screw-off lid for easy access during cleaning. This design feature allows the lid to be replaced with a different range of lids, enabling consumers to use the product for various purposes beyond simply carrying a cold beverage. The bottles were created using Adobe's suite of software, primarily utilizing the 3D tools in Illustrator and Dimension. Through this project, I was able to learn how to use 3D techniques to create a realistic visual representation of the bottle. Initially aiming for simplicity, I designed three classic bottles: black, white, and transparent.</p><p>‍</p><p>However, I eventually added a yellow bottle to provide a colourful example in case the company wanted to create a range of bottles with more vibrant colours.</p><p>‍</p>
