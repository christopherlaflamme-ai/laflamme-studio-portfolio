<p>Assembler l'intime is part of an auto-ethnographic approach in which object design becomes a tool for identity exploration. Inspired by two opposing family universes, one structured by a symbolic bourgeois aesthetic, the other by a logic of autarky and recycling, the series of pieces was created using only the material and technical resources available in the home.</p><p>‍</p><p>Each designed artifact expresses an affective relationship with space and memory, and questions traditional manufacturing codes. This approach articulates a singular formal language, rooted in personal experience. Through this research-creation process, the project highlights the potential of design as a sensitive medium, capable of drawing out intimate narratives and materializing identity-related tensions through form and material.</p>
