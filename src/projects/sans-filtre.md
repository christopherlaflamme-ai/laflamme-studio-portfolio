<p>Sans filtre is a series of three photographic posters produced as part of an academic project on social issues. Created with a minimum of digital editing, these posters denounce misinformation with a tone that is both absurd and critical. Each visual confronts unfounded popular beliefs with absurd analogies, illustrating just how logical some false assertions can seem... until you really think about them. </p><p>‍</p><p>The project takes a raw visual approach, aligned with a fictional collaboration with the Quebec government, to give institutional weight to the message: “If it doesn't make sense, it's probably misinformation.”</p>
