<p>In this project, the aim is to design a series of three typographic posters in red and white, promoting the Fire of Love concert/documentary at the Maison symphonique du grand Montréal. Drawing inspiration from the passionate journey of Katia and Maurice Krafft, renowned scientists and volcano enthusiasts, the posters will serve as visual invitations, capturing the essence of their exhilarating exploration. By ingeniously incorporating ASCII art, the outcome promises to be a captivating fusion of typography and imagery, compelling viewers to embark on a thrilling adventure of creation, destruction, and volcanic wonder.</p><p>‍</p>
